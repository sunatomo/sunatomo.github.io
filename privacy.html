Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

muse-tiktok-poster ("the App") is a personal tool that uploads draft videos to the TikTok account of its owner using the TikTok Content Posting API.

Information the App Accesses

When the account owner authorizes the App through TikTok Login Kit, the App receives an access token and basic profile information (such as open ID, username, and display name) for the authorized TikTok account, under the scopes user.info.basic and video.upload.

How Information Is Used

This information is used solely to upload draft videos to the owner's own TikTok inbox at the owner's direction. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose.

Sharing

The App does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. Access tokens are stored securely in the owner's private automation environment (GitHub Actions secrets) and are transmitted only to TikTok's official API endpoints.

Retention and Deletion

Access tokens can be revoked at any time by removing the App's authorization in the TikTok account settings. Upon revocation, stored tokens are deleted and can no longer be used.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the owner via the TikTok account @ikabugunko.
